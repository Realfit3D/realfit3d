## Execution Settings

- Stage 2 uses the existing GG registration objectives and iteration defaults.
- Stage 3 uses five epochs, 512-pixel AO/normal maps, and 64-sample Cycles baking.
  It holds out every twelfth sorted camera, including Cam005. The same camera was
  used during Stage 2, so this is only a Stage 3 camera holdout.
- Stage 4 holds out the last ten frames and fine-tunes for 1,000 updates from the
  official ContourCraft checkpoint: 500 registration-supervised updates and 500
  eight-step physics rollouts, over five epochs. Both paths use the Actor05 motion.
  This differs from a broad AMASS regularization experiment.
- The experiment-only `cmu_npz_smplx_preserve` loader preserves wrist pose values
  that the upstream loader clears. It is installed only in the isolated checkout.
- Dynamics validation initializes with two held-out meshes, then predicts eight
  frames. Distances are relative to Stage 2 registration, not scanned ground truth.

## Environment and Runtime Fixes

The GG/CC overlay uses the experiment's own reconstruction Python, not a teammate
environment. CUDA extensions are compiled for the 4090 (`sm_89`). Selected Blender
3.6 and PyMeshLab packages were copied from the existing 111 environment into the
own overlay; no shared package was modified.

- Warp 1.19 could not use driver 575/CUDA 12.9; Warp 1.9.1 initializes CUDA 12.8
  successfully and passed garment relaxation and train/rollout smoke tests.
- PyG extensions target PyTorch 2.5.1/cu121. RAPIDS is pinned to 24.12 and CuPy to
  the CUDA 12 package below version 14; NumPy remains below 2.
- CCCollisions was built from the 111 source based on
  `215195a7716810c2a6da069724409e5286a250d9`, including its local CUDA compatibility
  changes. Source files are saved in `stage234/sources/CCCollisions`.

  native render libraries that otherwise introduce a conflicting Embree library.
- GG path/Pillow fixes and Stage 3 checkpoint/preview fixes are recorded in the
  fork's bilingual change guides.
- RealFit3D conversion preserves full hand parameters, checks frame IDs before
  writing, selects one real sequence, and produces matching temporal partitions.

## Completed Run and Measurements

The 50-frame pipeline completed on November 10. All required commands returned 0.
Template registration completed in 73.99 seconds. Its measured output has zero
nonmanifold edges, 16 self-intersecting triangle pairs, and 12 vertices more than
2 mm inside the body. Maximum edge stretch is 1.426 relative to the cleaned
template, with p99 1.134. Visible contour overlays show local collar/cuff mismatch.

Stage 2 subsequently completed all 50 frames. The maximum self-intersection count
is 508 triangle pairs at local frame 48 (source 204). The maximum >2 mm body
penetration count is 117 vertices at frame 26 (source 182), and maximum edge
stretch is 2.200 at frame 14 (source 170). Topology remains manifold with unchanged
face/UV indices. Defect localization highlights chest folds/stretch and local
collar/shoulder penetration; visible silhouette overlap alone misses these defects.

`scene/mesh_model.py:196` defines bending, stretching, body collision, and virtual
edge energies, without an explicit garment self-intersection penalty. Its body
collision uses point-to-plane distances to selected body-face centers. The
independent mesh audit uses raycast signed distances instead. These code and
measurement results establish that the clean Stage 1 topology is not sufficient
to guarantee intersection-free Stage 2 geometry; they do not isolate every cause.

One-update Stage 3 training, frozen-checkpoint rendering, Stage 4 conversion,
two Stage 4 updates, and held-out simulation rendering all passed their isolated
smoke tests before the full run.

Stage 2 sequence registration took 31.34 minutes. Stage 3 completed five epochs
in 13.45 minutes, after 38.27 seconds of AO/normal baking. Mean masked PSNR over
50 frames is 29.319 dB at Cam007 and 28.311 dB at the Stage 3 holdout Cam005.
These garment-only diagnostics do not apply final body compositing. All 50 poses
were used by Stage 3, including the frames held out of dynamics training.

Stage 4 completed 1,000 updates in 63.06 minutes. On eight held-out predictions,
mean/p95/max vertex distance to registration is 13.655/31.695/66.728 mm. Maximum
self-intersections are 182 triangle pairs; maximum raycast penetration flags are
45 vertices. The 100-update checkpoint had 15.056 mm mean distance and the same
maximum collision counts. More updates reduced distance by approximately 9.3%
but did not remove the worst collision cases.

The separate full-clip rollout initializes from frames 156-157 and predicts the
remaining 48 frames. It includes training poses. Its mean/p95/max distance is
27.986/64.816/110.202 mm, with at most six self-intersection pairs and 18 raycast
penetration flags. Its start pose and rollout length differ from the held-out
test, so these runs do not isolate initialization quality alone.

On the eight held-out predictions, frozen Stage 3 weights produce mean masked
PSNR 29.688 dB on registered geometry and 18.648 dB on simulated geometry. The
camera and bake settings are fixed; geometry and derived AO/normal maps change.
This measures their combined effect, not a separate appearance-network ablation.

The actual GG renderer produced both the 10-frame held-out composite and the
50-frame full-clip composite. Identical-camera mesh rendering confirms that the
hem protrusion exists in Stage 2 geometry and remains after simulation; its brown
color comes from appearance rendering. It is not solely a Gaussian rendering
artifact. The cleaned Stage 1 template also has an irregular hem and initially
overlaps the fitted body at the chest. Zero self-intersections do not measure
boundary shape quality or garment/body fit.

The supplied body mesh has 32 boundary/nonmanifold edges and is not watertight.
Raycast signed-distance counts are collision indicators, not exact inside/outside
ground truth. At source frame 182, 117 Stage 2 vertices are flagged by raycasting;
88 are also inward by more than 2 mm according to closest-surface normals. At
source frame 205, those counts are 98/58 for registration and 8/1 for the full-clip
simulation. Detailed cross-checks are saved in `stage1_body_audit.json`.

Eight review MP4s decode completely with their expected frame counts at 25 fps.
Local downloads match the remote SHA256 hashes. Final checkpoints remain on NAS:
`output/Actor05_calibrated_clip/stage3/ckpt/net.pt` and
`ccraft_data/trained_models/finetuning/Actor05_calibrated_clip/step_0000046000.pth`.

Local tests: 65 tests in the lightweight environment, with three NumPy pose tests
skipped there; all eight conversion tests passed separately with NumPy available.
All 28 GG CPU tests passed, including three new regressions for unsigned RGB
export, small-image preview layout, and canonical end-of-epoch checkpoints.
